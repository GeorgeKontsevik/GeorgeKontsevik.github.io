**Equatorial** combines transport, climate and agriculture datasets to study accessibility across the equatorial belt. The Python workflow supports data acquisition, vector and raster processing, database-backed road-network analysis and reproducible research figures.

The figures below show crop-cluster origins, weekly precipitation scenarios and modelled changes in travel time to destinations.

[Code on GitHub](https://github.com/GeorgeKontsevik/equatorial)
