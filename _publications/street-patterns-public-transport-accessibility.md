Research in progress with **Yana Zorina**, under joint supervision by **George Kontsevik** and **Liubov Tupikina**.

This project investigates how street-network patterns influence the structure of public transport routes and accessibility to services. It develops a framework for multimodal transport route modelling that incorporates urban morphology into routing and mode choice.

The work asks how the geometry and connectivity of street networks shape the possibilities for improving service accessibility through public transport planning.

A related abstract, **“Computational Framework for Sustainable Transport Planning on Street Networks”**, was accepted for a poster presentation at NetSci 2026.
